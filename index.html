Your words. Three simple actions.

Clean

Remove HTML, unwanted formatting and invisible characters. Get plain text with normal spacing, keeping your words and punctuation.

☷

Optimize

Turn headings, paragraphs and bullet points into clean HTML. Review the structure in Preview, or inspect the tags in HTML.

⧉

Copy result

Copy plain text after Clean, or formatted text after Optimize. In the HTML view, copy the code for your CMS source editor.

Keep paragraphs preserves paragraph breaks when you use Clean. Turn it off for one continuous block of text. Clear empties the editor; Load example lets you try the tools.
